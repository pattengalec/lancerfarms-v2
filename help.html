Visitor

Free, no sign-in needed

Anyone can do everything in this section — no account, no login.

Request staff access

On index.html, the homepage
  1. Tap the button to request access (near the top of the homepage).
  2. Fill in your name and email.
  3. Choose the role you're requesting and add a short reason.
  4. Submit. An admin reviews requests from the admin panel — you'll be approved there before staff features unlock for your account.

Learn what grows here

learn.html, from the Visitor menu
  1. Open the drawer (the lines icon, top left) and tap Learn under Visitor.
  2. Browse what's currently growing and what each crop needs.
  3. The page supports multiple languages — check the language switch if English isn't your first choice.

Calculate how much soil a bed needs

do.html, from the Visitor menu
  1. Open Do from the drawer.
  2. Pick the bed you're filling.
  3. Enter the fill depth in inches.
  4. Pick your bag size.
  5. Read the result in cubic feet, already converted to bags for you.

Estimate when a crop will be ready

do.html, second calculator
  1. On the same Do page, scroll to "When will it be ready?"
  2. Pick the plant.
  3. Enter the date it was planted.
  4. Read the estimated ready date.

Browse farm photos

see.html, from the Visitor menu
  1. Open See from the drawer.
  2. Use the filter to narrow photos to a specific zone or bed, if you're looking for one spot in particular.
  3. Tap any photo to open it full-screen; swipe or tap the arrows to step through the rest.

Every photo here has already been reviewed by staff — see Upload & approve photos for how that pipeline works.

Leave a note for the farm

share.html, from the Visitor menu
  1. Open Share from the drawer.
  2. Type your note and submit it.
  3. The same page shows how many people have visited the site.

Watch the farm film

watch.html
  1. Open Watch from the footer or a direct link.
  2. Play the short film about the farm.
  3. Use the share button to copy a link or open your device's native share sheet if you want to send it to someone.

Learn what Grush is

about-grush.html
  1. Open About Grush from the footer's feature guide, or a direct link.
  2. Read what the software is and why the farm runs on it.

Support the developer

donate.html, under Support in the drawer
  1. Open the drawer, tap Support the Developer under Support.
  2. Read the notice — this supports the site's developer personally, not the farm itself, and it's not tax-deductible.
  3. Tap a preset amount, or Other to enter a custom one. This opens Venmo with the amount already filled in.
  4. Send it in Venmo.
  5. Back on the site, optionally fill in the receipt form and tap Generate receipt for your own records — then Print / Save as PDF if you want a copy.

Donate supplies to the farm

fund-the-farm.html, under Support in the drawer
  1. Open the drawer, tap Fund the Farm under Support.
  2. Check the list of what's currently needed — seeds, tools, equipment, growing supplies.
  3. Fill in your name, email, and what you'd like to give.
  4. Tap Send offer. Someone from the farm will reach out to arrange it.

A dedicated way to give money directly to the farm is marked coming soon on the same page. Until then, mention it in the offer form and the farm will follow up.

Staff

Sign-in required

Everything below needs an approved account. If you don't have one yet, see Request staff access above.

Sign in as staff

Any staff page — the sign-in prompt appears automatically
  1. Open any staff page (mustard-colored, e.g. app.html).
  2. Enter your email address at the sign-in prompt.
  3. Check your email for a sign-in link and tap it.
  4. You'll be returned to the page, signed in. This carries across every staff page — you won't need to sign in again per page.

Log farm activity

app.html?s=log — Log activity, under Staff in the drawer
Sign-in required
  1. Open the drawer, tap Log activity under Staff.
  2. Fill in what you did, where, and any notes.
  3. Submit — it's recorded immediately under your account.

Manage tasks

app.html?s=tasks — Tasks, under Staff in the drawer
Sign-in required
  1. Open the drawer, tap Tasks under Staff.
  2. Tap + to add a new task — give it a title, a bed/area if relevant, and set it to repeat if it's recurring.
  3. Tap an existing task to edit or remove it.
  4. Tap the checkmark to mark a task complete.

Upload & approve photos

app.html?s=photos — Photos, under Staff in the drawer
Sign-in required
  1. Open the drawer, tap Photos under Staff.
  2. Take or choose a photo, tag the bed or area it's from, and upload.
  3. It enters the moderation queue — it will not appear on the public See page until an operator approves it from the admin panel.

This is deliberate: nothing reaches visitors unreviewed.

Manage inventory

app.html?s=inventory — Inventory, under Staff in the drawer
Sign-in required
  1. Open the drawer, tap Inventory under Staff.
  2. Use the + / − buttons on any item to adjust quantity on the spot.
  3. Tap the camera icon on an item to attach or update its photo.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap + Add item for anything not already listed.

Manage growing areas & locations

app.html?s=areas — Locations, under Staff in the drawer
Sign-in required
  1. Open the drawer, tap Locations under Staff.
  2. Add, rename, or archive beds and other growing areas.
  3. These are the same areas that appear throughout the site — in Tasks, Inventory, the Garden Map, and bed lookup.

Diagnose a sick plant

triage.html, Plant triage under Tools in the drawer
  1. Open the drawer, tap Plant triage under Tools.
  2. Answer the prompts about what you're seeing on the plant — leaf color, pattern, location of the symptom, and so on.
  3. Read the likely cause and the recommended next action.

Calculate a mix, spray, or water demand

mixbench.html, Mix bench under Tools in the drawer
  1. Open the drawer, tap Mix bench under Tools.
  2. Use the Chemical stock tab to work out soil amendment, spray, or treatment quantities for a given bed size.
  3. Switch to the Water demand tab to calculate irrigation volume for a zone.

Plan an irrigation run

irrigation-bom.html, Irrigation under Tools in the drawer
  1. Open the drawer, tap Irrigation under Tools.
  2. Enter your measured supply and system settings.
  3. Lay out your bed groups.
  4. Read the priced parts list per zone, per bed, and garden-wide. Prices are editable — replace the defaults with your own invoice figures before treating the total as official.

Use the field manual

manual.html, Farm manual under Tools in the drawer
  1. Open the drawer, tap Farm manual under Tools.
  2. Tap a topic card — Soil & Amendments, Concrete & Hardscape, Irrigation & Water, Pest & Disease, Planting & Crops, or Tools & Equipment.
  3. Each topic opens its own calculator, reference guide, or checklist, depending on what it's marked as on the card.

Live bed-by-bed planting data lives on a separate page — see Farm data.

Place a pin on the garden map

garden-map.html, Garden Map under Staff in the drawer
Sign-in required
  1. Open the drawer, tap Garden Map under Staff.
  2. Tap a name from the "Not yet placed" list at the bottom.
  3. Tap that spot on the satellite map.
  4. The pin is saved immediately — no separate save step.
  5. To reposition an existing pin, tap it, then Move, drag it, and tap it again to lock in the new spot.
  6. To add something not already in the list, tap + New point not in this list, fill in a name and type, then tap the map.

Use how-to cards

howto.html, How-to cards under Staff in the drawer
  1. Open the drawer, tap How-to cards under Staff.
  2. Browse or search for the task you need.
  3. Open a card to see its numbered steps, written to be read standing up with dirty hands.

Build a research experiment

experiments.html, reached through Faculty & Research
Faculty sign-in required
  1. Open the drawer, tap Faculty & Research under Staff, then Experiments.
  2. Tap + New template and fill in the research question, background, design, materials, hypothesis, and procedure steps.
  3. Inside a template, tap + New section to create one semester or team's actual run of it — give it a label, term, and dates.
  4. Inside a section, add students, assign beds or bench areas, and log data readings as the experiment runs.
  5. Use Assign again on a finished section to carry the same areas forward into a new section next semester, without rebuilding the template.
  6. Export data as CSV or use Print report for a formal writeup.

Approve requests & manage crew

admin.html, Admin panel under Admin in the drawer
Admin sign-in required
  1. Open the drawer, tap Admin panel under Admin.
  2. Review pending access requests (see Request staff access) and approve or decline each one.
  3. Review the photo queue and approve or reject submissions before they go public.
  4. Manage the crew roster — add, rename, or deactivate accounts.
  5. Review submitted activity logs, and print activity logs when needed.
