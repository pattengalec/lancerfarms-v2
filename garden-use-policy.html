Garden Use Policy

Ground rules for anyone using the farm as a wellness space or an adopted growing area — short, and meant to be read before you start.

Purpose

Lancer Farms & Gardens is a working teaching garden. This policy covers casual use of the space — sitting, studying, reflecting, or tending an adopted bed — separate from coursework or research use, which follows its own faculty-supervised process.

Who this is for

Any CBU student. Adopting a growing area is limited to students living on campus, matching the pilot's current scope.

Hours & access

The farm is open during daylight hours, consistent with general campus grounds access. It is not staffed around the clock — the caretaker keeps regular hours, not 24/7 supervision.

Caring for an adopted bed

  • Tend your own bed; leave others' beds and plants alone unless invited.
  • Use only the farm's shared tools and irrigation as directed by the caretaker — ask before bringing your own equipment.
  • No pesticides, herbicides, or fertilizers beyond what the caretaker provides, without prior approval.
  • Harvest what you grow. If you step away for a season, let the caretaker know so the bed can be reassigned.

Shared-space courtesy

  • Keep the gazebo, grove, and paths clear for others to use.
  • Pack out any trash; leave the space as you found it.
  • Be mindful of noise — this space exists partly for quiet reflection.

Safety

Wear closed-toe shoes when working a bed. Report any injury, damaged equipment, or safety concern to the caretaker as soon as possible. The farm operates under the same photo-consent and visitor-safety process already in place for the site — adopting a bed doesn't change that.

This page is a working draft written to support a campus approval request. It is not a final or legally binding policy, and it hasn't been reviewed by CBU's official channels yet. Wording is expected to change before this is used for real.
