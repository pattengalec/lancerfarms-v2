Overview

Lancer Farms & Gardens is a student-run organic teaching garden at California Baptist University, located next to the historic Hawthorne House. It operates as a live campus site — not a static demonstration plot.

Research-ready infrastructure

The farm already runs on a digital operations layer, built in-house — not proposed, live today.

  • 🔑Per-bed QR records. Every bed has an individual digital record and physical QR sign, linking directly to that bed's data.
  • 📸Photo-documented logs. 59 photo records on file with an approval workflow, tied to specific beds and plantings.
  • 🌿Plant & trial tracking. 34 tracked plant profiles with planting windows, spacing, and stage timelines already in the database.
  • 📡Zoned drip irrigation. 3-zone controlled irrigation with measured supply rates, independent of hand-watering.

Experiments

A working tool, not a mockup — set up a trial, assign beds or bench spots, add students, and log data.

Create an experiment, assign it real beds or a custom bench spot, add students by school email with their preferred contact method, and log readings as the trial runs — with charts, CSV export, and a print-ready report built in.

Chad's parallel venture, Desert Fog Mycology, contributes direct substrate and fungal-cultivation expertise for a mycoremediation pilot specifically.
