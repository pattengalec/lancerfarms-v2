WHAT IT WAS FOR
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
A teaching model, not a diagnostic. triage.html walks you through
one sick plant and gives an answer. This was meant to build the
mental model underneath: that a plant is seven interlocking systems,
and trouble in one pulls on the others. You learn the shape of the
problem space, then triage becomes legible.

Visitor-facing description, as written on visitor.html:
  "An interactive model of the seven systems that keep a plant
   alive, and how trouble in one pulls on the others."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE SEVEN SYSTEMS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  1. Light
  2. Water
  3. Excretion
  4. Nutrition
  5. Parasitic
  6. Immune
  7. Respiration

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE SYNDROMES — where the teaching actually lives
Each names a set of systems that fail together, which is the
whole point: single-system thinking misses these.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Root rot complex
    systems: Water + Respiration + Immune
    Overwatering, airless soil, and opportunistic pathogens. The classic three-factor decline \u2014 treating any one alone will not stop it.

  Salt lockout
    systems: Excretion + Nutrition + Water
    Salts accumulate faster than water flushes them, and nutrients present in the soil stop being available.

  Drought–borer cascade
    systems: Water + Parasitic + Immune
    Water stress attracts boring and sucking pests, and their wounds admit pathogens the plant would otherwise resist.

  Carbon starvation
    systems: Light + Respiration + Nutrition
    Too little light to cover respiration, so reserves drain and uptake slows behind it.

INTERACTIONS THAT WERE DESIGNED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  - Tap a system to raise it and light up what it connects to
  - Drag to rotate, pinch to zoom
  - "Load a decline" — run a worked example
  - "Walk a real plant instead" — a Yes / No / Haven't looked
    survey that takes field observations and shows which systems
    are implicated
  - Connections warm when both ends are stressed

WHY IT FAILED, SO THE REBUILD DOESN'T REPEAT IT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  - It opened with new THREE.WebGLRenderer() at the top of the
    script. When that threw, every button below it never got
    wired. One failure killed the whole page.
  - It needed a 600KB 3D library from a CDN to teach a concept
    that is really a seven-node graph.
  - Nothing degraded. The survey and the text needed no 3D but
    died with it.

REBUILD NOTES
  - A seven-node graph in SVG or 2D canvas would carry the whole
    idea at a fraction of the weight, with no CDN dependency.
  - Wire the survey first, independently. It is the part that
    teaches; the diagram is the illustration.
  - The syndromes are the payload. Lead with them.
