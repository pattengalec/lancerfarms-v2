When will it be ready?

Only the annuals appear here. Trees, berries and perennials are not measured in days to maturity, so listing them would invite a false answer.

—

Days to maturity is the farm's own recorded estimate, and it is usually counted from transplant. Starting from seed adds two to four weeks, and a cool spring or short days will push it later still. Treat it as the earliest week to start checking, not a promise.
