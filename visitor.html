Welcome to the Farm

Lancer Farms & Gardens is a student-run organic teaching garden at California Baptist University, located next to the historic Hawthorne House. The garden includes raised beds, a citrus grove, and a composting station — all maintained by environmental science students and faculty.

What's Growing

The garden rotates seasonal vegetables, herbs, and native plants year-round. Current crops and field data are tracked live on the farm data page.

Farm Manual

Watering schedules, soil amendments, pest management, and planting calendars are documented in the Farm Manual — built and maintained by the caretaker team.
