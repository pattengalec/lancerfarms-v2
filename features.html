Feature guide

Everything on this site.

One farm, two doors. The visitor side is free and always will be. The staff side is Grush, the software the crew runs the garden on. Here is what is behind each one.

The visitor side

Free. Always.

No login, no account, nothing to sign. Anyone can walk up and read the farm.

English · Español · Français · Português
Welcome & orientation What the garden is, who runs it, and what you are looking at when you stand in it. visitor.html Live farm data What is planted in which bed, what was sown when, and what has come out of the ground. Updated by the crew as they work, not typed up later. data.html Seasonal almanac Sun, frost dates and the growing calendar for Riverside, zone 9b. What to plant now, and what is already too late. almanac.html How-to cards Short, single-task guides for the jobs that come up in a garden. Written to be read standing up with dirty hands. howto.html Field manual The long-form reference behind the cards — methods, reasoning and the things that go wrong. manual.html Bed pages Every bed carries a QR code on a stake. Scan it and you get that bed's history. Visitors see what is growing; staff see the log. bed.html

The staff side

Powered by Grush.

The management layer the crew actually runs on. Same site, same data, different door.

Row-level security · Roles
Staff field tool Log work, tasks and supplies from a phone, outdoors, in gloves. The screen everything else is built around. app.html Admin & approvals Crew roster, access requests, and the queue where submitted work gets reviewed before it becomes part of the record. admin.html Plant triage Walk a sick plant through symptoms and get to a likely cause and a next action, instead of guessing. triage.html Mix bench Work out soil, amendment and solution mixes to the volume actually needed. No arithmetic on the back of a seed packet. mixbench.html Irrigation planning Lay out a run and get the parts list and quantities that come with it. irrigation-bom.html
Photo pipeline. Photos taken in the field upload from the phone, land in a moderation queue, and only appear publicly once an operator approves them. Nothing reaches the visitor side unreviewed.
Honest blanks. Where the farm has no data, the site says so. It never fills a gap with a plausible guess — a wrong planting date is worse than an empty one.
