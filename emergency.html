What to tell the 911 operator

"I'm at California Baptist University, 8432 Magnolia Avenue, Riverside."

"I'm at the Lancer Farms garden, behind the Colony apartments, next to the Hawthorne House."

"The nearest vehicle access is FILL IN."

"Someone will meet you at FILL IN."

"GPS coordinates are 33.9281, −117.4301."

Then stay on the line. Give what happened, how many people, and whether anyone is unconscious, bleeding heavily, or not breathing. Do not hang up until they say you can.

Send someone to meet the ambulance if there is anyone else with you. This garden is not visible from the street and the address is shared by the whole campus — an unmet responder can lose several minutes finding a 160-acre site's back corner.

Call Safety Services after 911, not before. They can open gates and guide the engine in, but they are not a substitute for dispatch.

The two FILL IN lines above are blanks on purpose. Nobody should invent a meeting point for an ambulance — put the real gate and the real corner in, then print this page and pin it in the shed.

Incident report

For an injury, a near miss, or anything involving a person. Fill this out the same day — detail decays fast. It goes to the farm's records, not to emergency services.
